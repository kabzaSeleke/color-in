Who We Are

Built on

Ngutyana Construction and Project Management is a 100% black-owned company offering a full spectrum of construction and contracting services — from civil and building construction, roadworks, and plant hire to opencast mining services across South Africa.

Our primary goal is to satisfy our customers' needs in the most cost-effective and efficient manner possible. Our experienced staff assess proposed contracts carefully to advise on the most economical approach to complete each project.
